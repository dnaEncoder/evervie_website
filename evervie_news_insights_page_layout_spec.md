# Evervie News & Insights Page
## Visual Layout and Content Specification

## 1. Page Purpose

The News & Insights page is a central editorial destination for:

- Evervie-authored articles and leadership perspectives
- Company viewpoints and strategic commentary
- Healthcare and sector insights
- Market and economic trends
- Policy, regulation, investment, and sustainability topics

The page should clearly separate **Evervie’s own thinking** from broader **market-oriented analysis**, while allowing both content types to live within one searchable editorial system.

The primary journey is:

```text
Discover the lead insight
→ Explore featured articles
→ Filter or search the complete archive
→ Open an individual article
→ Subscribe for future insights
```

---

## 2. Overall Page Flow

```text
Global Header
Breadcrumb
Editorial Hero + Featured Article
Featured Insights
Latest Articles Archive
Supporting Sidebar
Newsletter Subscription
Global Footer
```

The page moves from a highly curated editorial introduction into a more functional article archive.

---

## 3. Global Header

Use the existing Evervie website header.

The **Newsroom** or **News & Insights** navigation item should appear active.

Below the header, place a breadcrumb:

```text
Home / Newsroom / News & Insights
```

The breadcrumb sits within the main page container and aligns with the content below.

---

## 4. Editorial Hero Section

### Overall layout

Use a two-column composition:

```text
[ Left page introduction ]       [ Right featured article ]
```

Suggested desktop proportions:

```text
Left: approximately 38–42%
Right: approximately 58–62%
```

The two areas align vertically but perform different functions:

- The left introduces the editorial platform.
- The right promotes the most important current article.

### Left introduction area

The left side contains:

```text
Eyebrow
Large page headline
Short supporting copy
Search field
```

#### Eyebrow

```text
NEWS & INSIGHTS
```

#### Main headline

```text
Ideas. Perspectives.
Insights that matter.
```

The headline is split across two or three controlled lines.

#### Supporting copy

```text
Stay informed with our perspectives on markets, industries, healthcare, and the ideas shaping the future.
```

#### Search field

Place a large search input beneath the introduction.

Placeholder:

```text
Search articles, topics or authors
```

The search field should include a search icon on the right.

It should search across:

- Article titles
- Summaries
- Categories
- Topics
- Author names
- Keywords

### Right featured article

The right side contains one large landscape image.

The featured article information is overlaid toward the bottom of the image.

The overlay contains:

```text
Article category
Article title
Publication date
Reading time
Read more action
```

Example structure:

```text
MARKET OUTLOOK

India’s Growth Momentum:
Opportunities in a Changing Global Landscape

22 May 2026 · 6 min read

Read more →
```

The article title should be the dominant element within the image overlay.

The complete visual should act as one large clickable article link.

A subtle darkening treatment behind the text may be used to maintain readability over different images.

---

## 5. Featured Insights Section

### Section header

Place a horizontal section header beneath the hero:

```text
Featured Insights                         View all featured →
```

The heading sits on the left.

The text link on the right opens the complete featured-content archive.

### Featured article layout

Display three featured articles in one row on desktop:

```text
[ Featured Article 01 ] [ Featured Article 02 ] [ Featured Article 03 ]
```

Each article contains:

```text
Landscape image
Category label
Article headline
Publication date
Reading time
Directional arrow
```

Example:

```text
MARKET TRENDS

Decoding Market Volatility:
Key Factors Investors Should Watch

18 May 2026 · 5 min read
```

The image occupies the upper portion of the module.

The article information sits beneath it.

The arrow appears at the bottom-right and reinforces that the entire item is clickable.

The three items can represent different content territories, such as:

- Market Trends
- Sector Insight
- Economic Perspective

Avoid repeating nearly identical topics across all three featured positions.

---

## 6. Latest Articles Section

### Overall layout

The lower content area uses an asymmetric two-column structure:

```text
[ Main article archive ]       [ Supporting sidebar ]
```

Suggested desktop proportions:

```text
Main archive: approximately 72–76%
Sidebar: approximately 24–28%
```

### Main archive header

The main column begins with:

```text
Latest Articles
```

Below the heading is a horizontal set of topic filters.

Suggested filters:

```text
All
Market Trends
Economy
Sector Insights
Company Updates
Healthcare
Sustainability
Policy & Regulation
```

The list may scroll horizontally when more categories are added.

The active filter should be visibly distinguished.

Selecting a filter updates the article list below.

### Article row structure

Articles are displayed as horizontal editorial rows rather than large repeated cards.

Recommended structure:

```text
[ Thumbnail ] [ Article content ] [ Date / reading time / bookmark ]
```

#### Thumbnail

Use a compact landscape image on the left.

The image should maintain a consistent aspect ratio across all rows.

#### Article content

The central area contains:

```text
Category
Article title
Short summary
Optional author
```

Example:

```text
MARKET TRENDS

Global Markets in 2026: What Lies Ahead?

An overview of key global macroeconomic factors and their potential impact on markets in the year ahead.
```

#### Metadata and utility area

The right side contains:

```text
Publication date
Reading time
Bookmark / save icon
```

Example:

```text
20 May 2026
6 min read
Bookmark
```

Use a divider between each article row.

Do not place every article inside a large independent card.

### Load more action

Place a centred action beneath the archive:

```text
Load more articles
```

This may progressively reveal additional articles or request the next page of results.

---

## 7. Supporting Sidebar

The right sidebar contains three compact supporting modules.

These should assist content discovery without competing with the main article archive.

### 7.1 Popular Topics

#### Heading

```text
Popular Topics
```

Display topic links as small selectable tags.

Examples:

```text
Market Outlook
Economy
Sector Insights
Investing
Healthcare
Sustainability
Policy & Regulation
```

Clicking a topic filters or opens the corresponding archive.

The number and names of topics should respond to the published content.

### 7.2 About News & Insights

#### Heading

```text
About News & Insights
```

Suggested copy:

```text
Our insights bring together thoughtful analysis, expert perspectives, and in-depth research on healthcare, markets, sectors, and the forces shaping our world.
```

Include one contextual link:

```text
Learn more about our approach →
```

This can link to an editorial-policy page, company overview, or explanation of Evervie’s thought-leadership programme.

### 7.3 Sidebar Subscription

#### Heading

```text
Stay Informed
```

Suggested copy:

```text
Subscribe to receive our latest insights and perspectives.
```

The form contains:

```text
Email field
Submit arrow
Consent checkbox
Consent statement
```

Example consent text:

```text
I agree to receive communications from Evervie.
```

This compact form is useful while the user is browsing the article archive.

---

## 8. Full-Width Newsletter Section

Near the bottom of the page, include a larger full-width subscription band.

### Layout

```text
[ Newsletter icon ] [ Heading and supporting copy ] [ Email field and submit ]
```

Suggested copy:

```text
Never miss an update

Subscribe to get the latest insights delivered to your inbox.
```

The email input and submit action sit on the right side on desktop.

This newsletter band should be more prominent than the compact sidebar subscription and act as the final conversion point before the footer.

---

## 9. Footer

Use the existing Evervie global footer.

The footer can include:

```text
Evervie logo
Privacy Policy
Terms of Use
Sitemap
Contact Us
Copyright
Scroll-to-top control
```

Do not introduce a unique footer only for the News & Insights page.

## 10. Content Categories and Editorial Logic

The page should support both company-authored content and broader market commentary.

### Evervie Perspectives

Articles written from the organisation’s point of view.

Examples:

- Evervie strategy
- Building specialised healthcare platforms
- Operating-company development
- Patient access
- Leadership perspectives
- Long-term healthcare investment

### Market Trends

Broader developments that affect industries, companies, and investors.

Examples:

- Global markets
- Interest rates
- Inflation
- Capital flows
- Investment trends

### Healthcare Insights

Analysis connected to Evervie’s operating context.

Examples:

- Speciality-care demand
- Healthcare access
- Oncology trends
- Renal-care infrastructure
- Diagnostics
- Elder-care development

### Sector Insights

Industry-specific analysis outside purely corporate updates.

### Economy

Macroeconomic and regional-economic commentary.

### Policy & Regulation

Relevant regulatory and policy changes.

### Sustainability

Healthcare sustainability, responsible growth, and long-term institutional development.

The system should clearly label each article so readers understand whether it is:

- Company commentary
- Market analysis
- Sector research
- Leadership opinion
- Corporate update

Each article should be capable of linking to a complete article-detail page containing:

```text
Category
Article title
Subtitle or summary
Hero image
Publication date
Author
Author designation
Reading time
Article body
Pull quotes
Supporting images
Charts or diagrams
Source references, where applicable
Related articles
Share controls
Previous / next article navigation

```

Market-oriented articles should clearly distinguish:

- Factual information
- Evervie interpretation
- Third-party sources
- Forward-looking opinions

## 12. Interaction Notes

### Search

- Search should update results after submission or with a restrained debounce.
- Search results should replace or filter the Latest Articles archive.
- A clear reset action should return the complete archive.

### Category filters

- Selecting a category updates the archive.
- Filters should be shareable through the page URL where practical.
- The active filter remains visible while browsing.

### Article bookmarking

The bookmark icon can:

- Save articles locally
- Connect to a user account
- Be omitted if no saved-content system is planned

Do not show the icon unless it performs a real action.

### Featured content

- The hero feature should be selected editorially through the CMS.
- Featured Insights may contain a maximum of three or four visible articles.
- Do not automatically feature the newest article unless no editorial selection exists.

## 13. Responsive Behaviour

### Desktop

```text
Two-column hero
Three-column featured insights
Main archive + right sidebar
Horizontal article rows
Full-width newsletter band
```

### Tablet

```text
Hero may remain two columns
Featured articles become two columns
Sidebar moves below or beside a narrower archive
Article metadata remains right-aligned where space allows
```

### Mobile

Stack the page vertically:

```text
Breadcrumb
Page introduction
Search
Featured article
Featured Insights
Category filters
Latest article rows
Popular Topics
About News & Insights
Subscription
Footer
```

On mobile:

- The featured article becomes a vertical image-and-copy block.
- Featured Insights display one per row or in a horizontal swipe system.
- Article rows stack the thumbnail above or beside the content.
- Date and reading time move beneath the summary.
- Filter chips scroll horizontally.
- The sidebar becomes part of the normal page flow.
- The newsletter form stacks vertically.

---

## 14. Content Behaviour

The layout should adapt to available content.

- Articles without images use a consistent editorial fallback.
- Missing reading times should be omitted.
- Long headlines wrap without breaking row alignment.
- Articles without authors do not reserve empty author space.
- The latest archive can support pagination or progressive loading.
- Categories with no content should not be shown as active filters.
- Featured articles should not also be repeated immediately as the first archive entries unless intentionally required.
- Market-trend content should include source attribution where relevant.
- Old or superseded market analysis may display its original publication date clearly.

---

## 15. Backend Content Note

All article information—including titles, categories, authors, dates, summaries, images, reading times, topics, featured status, article bodies, and related content—should be populated dynamically from the existing backend or CMS.

The frontend should define only:

- Page structure
- Article presentation
- Search and filter interactions
- Responsive behaviour
- Newsletter forms
- Loading, empty, and error states

No article-specific information should be hardcoded into the frontend.
